Our Story

North Star Bakery began with a simple goal: to create a welcoming neighborhood bakery where people could enjoy fresh, carefully made food. What started as a small community-focused bakery has grown into a place where neighbors meet, families celebrate, and commuters can start their day with something freshly baked.

We continue to focus on traditional baking methods, small batches, and recipes that make our customers feel at home.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We believe good baking starts with good ingredients. Whenever possible, we work with local suppliers and choose quality flour, grains, fruits, dairy products, and other ingredients.

Seasonal ingredients also help us create rotating products that reflect the flavors of the community around us.
